# Editing Your Website — A Simple Guide

Your website now has a built-in editor. You can change the words and photos
yourself — no code, no developer needed. This guide walks you through it.

## Getting in

1. Go to **https://unfilteredrays.com/studio**
2. Log in with the email you were invited with (Google or email link).

You'll see a list down the left side: **Site Settings, Home Page, About Page,
Services Page, FAQ Page, Gallery Page, Contact Page.** Each one matches a part of
your website.

## Making a change

1. Click the page you want to edit on the left.
2. Click into any field and type your change.
3. When you're happy, click the **Publish** button (bottom right).

That's it. Your change goes live on the website within about a minute.

> Nothing is live until you press **Publish**. Feel free to click around and
> explore — you can't break anything, and you can always undo.

## Swapping a photo

1. Open the page with the photo (e.g. **Gallery Page** or **Home Page**).
2. Click the existing image.
3. Click **Remove**, then drag in or upload your new photo.
4. Add a short **Alt text** — a few words describing the photo (helps Google and
   accessibility). Example: *"Couple laughing at the photo booth."*
5. Press **Publish**.

### Adding or removing gallery photos

On the **Gallery Page**, scroll to **Gallery photos**:
- **Add:** click "Add item", upload a photo, add alt text.
- **Remove:** click the three dots next to a photo → Remove.
- **Reorder:** drag photos into the order you want.

Then **Publish**.

## Common things you might change

| You want to… | Go to… |
|---|---|
| Update a price | **Services Page → Collections** |
| Edit a question or answer | **FAQ Page → Questions & answers** |
| Change your email or Instagram | **Site Settings** |
| Update the homepage headline | **Home Page → Hero** |
| Swap gallery photos | **Gallery Page** |
| Reword the About story | **About Page → Paragraphs** |

## A few tips

- **Always press Publish** — edits aren't live until you do.
- **Undo:** the editor keeps a history. Use the "Review changes" / revert option
  if you change your mind.
- **Don't worry about layout** — colors, fonts, and spacing are handled for you.
  You're only changing words and photos.
- **Photos:** use good-quality images. They're automatically resized for the web.

## If something looks off

If a change doesn't appear after a couple of minutes, refresh the website page.
If it's still not right, undo your change in the Studio and reach out to your
developer.
